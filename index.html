About me

I sit where creativity meets commercial thinking — I design things that look great, and I make sure they move the numbers too.

I'm a self-taught designer, now studying a Marketing Level 4 course at EMA Training in Derby. I've run social media for local football, helped launch a personal wellness brand, and today look after social media, events and exhibitions as a Sales & Marketing Executive.
